Explanation

Step 1: Compute all possible sums of size k using a sliding window.

Step 2: For each index, record the index of the maximum sum subarray from the left side.

Step 3: Similarly, for each index, record the index of the maximum sum subarray from the right side.

Step 4: Iterate over possible middle subarrays, combining left + middle + right, and choose the combination that gives the maximum total sum.

✅ Time Complexity: O(n)
✅ Space Complexity: O(n)
